A LITTLE HELP BEFORE YOU START

Curious?
That's a good start.

I'm new to dance. Where do I start?＋

Start by telling us you're a beginner in the enquiry form. Ask the studio which current batch suits your experience, and what your first session will involve.

How do I check class timings and fees?＋

Choose “Fees and timings” in the WhatsApp enquiry form. Share your preferred days or times so the studio can help you find an available option.

Can I visit the studio before joining?＋

Select “Studio visit” in the form and ask for a suitable time. Use the map below for directions once your visit is confirmed.
